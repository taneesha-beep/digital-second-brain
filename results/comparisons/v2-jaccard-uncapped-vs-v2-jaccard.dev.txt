  What this says: the two runs rank identically at k=8. It is NOT evidence
  that the two retrievers are equivalent — look at the same pair at a larger k
  before concluding anything. No bootstrap can distinguish two vectors that are
  the same vector, and reporting a p-value here as though it had resolved
  something would be the failure this case was chosen to catch.

6. HOW THE RANDOMNESS IS PINNED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  prng                 mulberry32, seeded — the same generator build-splits.js
                       uses at 1.4. Math.random() cannot be seeded in Node, and a
                       p-value that changes between runs is not a result.
  seed                 20260804
  resamples            10000
                       chosen against the sampling error it estimates, not by
                       convention: at p ~= 0.05 the Monte Carlo standard error is
                       sqrt(0.05 * 0.95 / B) = 0.0022 here, which resolves the 0.05
                       threshold to about ± 0.004 — enough to decide significance,
                       not enough to justify a third decimal. The se is printed
                       above so that is visible rather than trusted.
  interval             percentile, over the 10000 resampled means, as a pair of
                       order statistics at index floor(alpha/2 * B) and its mirror
                       B-1-that. Symmetric by construction, so comparing A against
                       B and B against A give exactly mirrored intervals — the
                       registry accepts a registered pair in either direction, so
                       both orders will be run. The obvious nearest-rank convention
                       is off by one order statistic under that swap; a mirror test
                       caught it, worth ~2e-4 on real dev data.
  p-value              centred (shifted) bootstrap ASL — differences shifted to
                       mean zero under H0, then |mean*| >= |observed| counted, with
                       the (1+r)/(B+1) convention. Smucker, Allan & Carterette
                       (2007). NOT the interval inversion PRIMER §5.5 describes;
                       that reading is the raw count below.
  resamples favouring  A 0   B 0   tied 10000
                       PRIMER §5.5's "9,800 of 10,000 still ahead" reading, under
                       its own name. Deliberately not labelled a second p-value:
                       two p-values in one report is an invitation to quote the
                       smaller one.
