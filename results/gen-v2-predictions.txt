WHERE 2048 CAME FROM — AND IT IS NOT A ROUND NUMBER
==============================================================================

  The 14 truncated examQs calls are CENSORED: their true demand is not
  observable, only extrapolable. Two estimators were built from the 5.3
  ledger, from how many answers each call had begun before dying:

    A  last begun item counted COMPLETE (biased low)   max demand 1638
    B  last begun item counted HALF-WRITTEN            max demand 1944

  Decomposed, over 30 seeds:

    examQs content need    p50 792   p90 1480   p95 1804   max 1840
    examQs reasoning       p50 176   p90  323   p95  503   max  737

  2048 is the SMALLEST value clearing both estimators. It covers 30/30 under
  the conservative one with 5% headroom.

  It deliberately does NOT cover the worst-case pairing bound of 2577
  (max content 1840 + max reasoning 737). Those maxima occurred on DIFFERENT
  seeds, so pairing them is a bound nothing observed. Accepting that residual
  is what makes prediction 4 below falsifiable: near-zero examQs truncation is
  predicted, NOT certainly zero.

  Smallest-sufficient is the right criterion for a CAP, because every token of
  headroom above the clearing point is latency paid on exactly the calls being
  repaired — see prediction 12.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME CONCLUDE THE FIX DID NOT WORK
==============================================================================

  Pre-committed, so it cannot be rationalised after the numbers land.

  1. examQs conformance lands INSIDE [40%, 62%]. §28.8 measured 9 of 28 examQs
     cells flipping on a re-draw, so that band is indistinguishable from noise
     at n=1. Landing in it means the ceiling was not the binding defect.

  2. examQs truncation stays ABOVE ~20%. That would mean the censored-demand
     extrapolation is wrong in KIND, not in degree.

  3. flashcards or concepts MOVE AT ALL, in either direction. They sat at 100%
     with 0% truncation and the ceiling never bound them. If they move,
     max_tokens was not the only thing that changed — either a second variable
     leaked in, or THE MODEL DRIFTED BEHIND THE STRING. This is the built-in
     control for §28.9's unpinnable-model problem and it costs nothing.

  4. Mean output on summarize/flashcards/concepts moves by more than 10%.
     Same control, continuous version.


PRE-COMMITTED SPENDING RULE
==============================================================================

  Groq free tier is 200,000 tokens per DAY and NO x-ratelimit-* header exposes
  it (§28.6). 5.3 died on that cap. The balance remaining on any given day is
  not observable, so the run is capped rather than paced.

    --max-calls 150, hard stop at the balanced first pass, resumable ledger.

  IF examQs lands OUTSIDE [40%, 62%]  -> the first pass is sufficient, buy
                                         nothing more.
  IF examQs lands INSIDE  [40%, 62%]  -> buy ONE examQs-only second pass
                                         (30 calls, ~40k tokens) to separate
                                         "no effect" from "noise".

  Committed here so an adaptive decision is not a post-hoc one.


WHAT WILL SURPRISE ME
==============================================================================

  My hit rate is worst when reasoning about SHAPE rather than magnitude —
  §28.10's gradient-versus-cliff error cost four predictions at once, spent
  once per feature. Four candidates, most likely first:

  1. REASONING TOKENS SCALE WITH THE BUDGET RATHER THAN THE CONTENT. §28.7
     measured them at 16.6% of the 1024 ceiling. If a reasoning model expands
     its chain to fill available space, doubling the ceiling partly feeds
     REASONING rather than content and the fix underdelivers. Falsifiable:
     examQs reasoning mean should stay ~215 if content-driven, or climb toward
     ~430 if budget-driven. THIS IS THE ONE I WOULD BET ON.

  2. THE RESIDUAL examQs FAILURES WILL NOT BE TRUNCATION. With the ceiling
     lifted, the taxonomy underneath is essentially unmeasured — element-shape
     and cardinality were masked. §28's cause table had exactly ONE
     non-truncation failure to reason from.

  3. eli5 MAY NOT GO TO ZERO. examQs has a hard target (5 items); prose has no
     natural stopping point, so a bigger ceiling may buy a longer ramble that
     truncates again.

  4. THE LATENCY BREACH MAY BE WORSE THAN LINEAR. R^2 0.974 was fitted
     entirely under 1024 tokens; prediction 12 extrapolates past the range
     that produced it.
