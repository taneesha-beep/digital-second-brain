6a. GRADE COMPOSITION — is a "linked" judgment a relevance judgment at all?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ADDED AFTER THE HAND READ, AND THE PREDICTION WAS WRITTEN BEFORE IT RAN.
  Case 52209 ("How do I pick the best fillet mignon at the supermarket?") is
  judged against two documents about SALTING a steak, which is not what the
  question asks. §2.1: LinkTypeId 1 records that an answer or comment
  REFERENCED another question — a citation, not a claim that the two questions
  are about the same thing. LinkTypeId 3, grade 2, is a duplicate closure, and
  that one IS a same-question claim. If the distinction is real, queries whose
  key contains a grade 2 should fail markedly less often. Predicted before
  running: a gap of 10 to 20 points.

    key composition          queries   zero pop   zero rate   mean nDCG@8
    ------------------------------------------------------------------
    grade 1 only (linked)       1766        872       49.4%      0.297554
    any grade 2 (duplicate)      538        167       31.0%      0.423199

8. WHAT THIS FILE CANNOT SUPPORT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The hand-read categories are counts out of 20 in four cells of 5. They
    are NOT frequencies over the corpus and must never be reweighted into
    one: at n=5 per cell the variance of any projected share is enormous, and
    a projected percentage would be the most quotable and least defensible
    number in the writeup. Phase 7.1 takes section 3, not section 7.
  - REACHABLE is a lower bound. The run files stop at rank 10, so a judged
    document a rung would have found at rank 11 counts as unreachable here.
  - C3 is not a statement that a document is unfindable. It says the six
    rungs on this ladder do not find it in ten slots.
  - Nothing here measures whether an UNJUDGED retrieved document is relevant.
    That is §5.1's open problem, its only fix is pooling and hand-labelling
    (§5.4 option 2), and it is not done.
  - Dev only. Nothing here is a test-set result and nothing here was checked
    against test.
