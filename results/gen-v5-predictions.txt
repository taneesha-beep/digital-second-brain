PHASE 5.4 — PREDICTIONS, WRITTEN BEFORE THE FIRST gen-v5 API CALL
================================================================================

Committed in its own commit so the ordering is checkable in git, the same way
results/gen-predictions.txt (5.3) and results/gen-v2-predictions.txt (5.5) were.

Priors, so the hit rate below is read against something: 5.1 scored 3 right /
3 wrong / 1 untestable, 5.5 finished 9 right / 6 wrong / 0 untestable, 5.3 was
9 of 16, the README pass 4 of 6, and 4.6 5 of 10.

WHAT IS BEING PREDICTED. gen-v5 is Study Pack over the 30 seeds of the 5.2
golden set, n=1, one call per seed, through the LIVE services/studyPack.service.js.
Every band below is over those 30 calls, or over the ~420 items they return.

n IS DECLARED HERE AND NOT SET BY THE QUOTA. 5.3 stopped at 234 of 330 cells and
5.5 at 78 of 150, and in both cases what the run could afford chose n after the
fact (§28.6). 30 calls at ~2,195 ACTUAL tokens is ~66,000 of a 200,000/day
organisation cap — about a third of a day (§30.1's cost model). If the cap
refuses partway, the coverage is reported as partial and no band below is
scored on a fraction of the set.

TWO OF THESE ARE WHERE I EXPECT TO BE WRONG, AND WHY.

#1, TRUNCATION. §30.9 records `max_tokens: 2048` as INHERITED, NOT DERIVED —
§29.2 argued 2048 as smallest-sufficient from measured `examQs` demand, and no
such measurement exists for a study pack. The one live call at §30.8 spent
57.9% of its completion on REASONING against §28.7's ~17% on single notes, on a
608-token, five-note prompt. A golden cluster is ~1,412 prompt tokens across up
to nine notes and asks the model to RELATE them rather than summarise one, so
the reasoning cost should go up, not down. If #1 lands high, study-pack shape
conformance is ceiling-dominated and §28's headline defect has reproduced itself
on a new surface one phase after being declared fixed — with 5.5's residual 3.3%
`examQs` truncation at 2048 as the warning shot.

#8, THE NULL GAP. There is no prior for this anywhere in the project. It is the
number that decides whether citation SUPPORT means anything: if a claim scores
0.60 against the note it cites and 0.55 against every other note in the same
prompt, the citation carries almost no information and the headline support
figure hides that. It is also the number I have the most reason to want to be
large, which is exactly why the band is written down first.


WHAT THESE NUMBERS CANNOT BE, WHATEVER THEY COME OUT AT.

NO NOISE FLOOR EXISTS FOR ANY OF THEM. n=1 per seed measures BETWEEN-SEED
variation only. §28.8 measured 32.1% of `examQs` cells flipping verdict on a
re-draw at this temperature, and nothing here establishes the equivalent for a
study pack. So a gen-v5 rate may be compared against nothing — there is no
before, and no re-draw. Any future comparison that moves one of these numbers
has to buy its own repeats first.

CITATION SUPPORT IS A PROXY AND IS LABELLED ONE. It is lexical containment of
the claim's terms in the cited note's terms. A correct paraphrase sharing no
vocabulary scores zero and is NOT a hallucination; a claim built from generic
cooking vocabulary scores high against ANY cooking note. Both directions are
real and 5.6's judge is what actually answers the question.

THE SEEDS ARE STACK EXCHANGE QUESTIONS SHAPED AS NOTES. §12.2's gap, ninth
instance: the "<=500-note user slice" the app retrieves over has no referent on
this corpus.
