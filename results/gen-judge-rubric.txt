================================================================================

ROADMAP 5.6 requires the rubric to be committed verbatim, and this is that

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SYSTEM MESSAGE, VERBATIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You grade whether a CLAIM is supported by a PASSAGE.

Judge the claim ONLY against the passage in front of you. Ignore anything you
know from elsewhere: a claim that is true in the world but absent from the
passage is not supported by it. The passage may or may not be where the claim
came from.

A claim has two parts. The first names what it is about; the second is the
assertion being graded. For a question-and-answer pair the answer is the
assertion. For a term-and-definition pair the definition is the assertion.

2 SUPPORTED   every factual assertion in the claim is stated in the passage, or
              follows directly from what the passage states.
1 PARTIAL     the passage is about the claim's subject and supports part of the
              assertion, but at least one part of it is not in the passage.
0 UNSUPPORTED the passage does not support the claim: it contradicts it, or it
              is about something else, or the substance of the assertion is
              simply absent.

Wording will differ. Judge meaning, not vocabulary: a correct paraphrase that
shares no words with the passage is still supported. A claim that only names a
topic the passage covers, without asserting what the passage asserts, is 1. A
passage that merely ASKS about the claim's subject without answering it does
not support an answer, and is 0.

Reply with exactly one line: a single digit 2, 1 or 0, then a space, then at
most 12 words of reason. No other text.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE USER MESSAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  PASSAGE
  <title>
  <body>

  CLAIM
  <q + " " + a, or term + " " + definition>

  VERDICT:

PASSAGE BEFORE CLAIM, deliberately. The other order invites the model to read
the claim first and then scan for confirmation, which is the shape of
confirmation bias and the thing a groundedness judge exists to resist.

WHAT THE JUDGE IS NEVER SHOWN: the integer label the generator emitted, any
other note from the same pack, the slot name, the seed id, the quintile, or
whether this passage is the one the item cited. Both conditions are
structurally identical at the model's input, which is what makes the null a
null rather than an assertion that it is one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EMISSION ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Items are sorted by a fractional within-stratum position, so ANY PREFIX holds
each stratum in proportion to its size — a partial run is a SAMPLE of the 322,
not a corner of it. Round-robin was rejected: the strata run 48 down to 18, so
equal-per-stratum would over-represent Q5 relative to the population the
headline rate is about. An item's two conditions are emitted back to back, so
a stop never leaves a cited verdict without its null. Human-labelled items sort
first within their stratum, so kappa is available early rather than last.

§32.8 recorded that gen-v5 had no mechanism protecting its stratification and
got a balanced partial set by luck, and drew the lesson that the protection has
to be re-derived for whatever axis a new harness stratifies on. This is that.
