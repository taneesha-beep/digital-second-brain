8. WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
   - NOT an endpoint latency. No Mongo, no network, no serialisation on the
     wire, no browser layout. GET /api/graph/global pays all four.
   - NOT a measurement of a notebook. Stack Exchange documents shaped as
     Notes; §12.2 and §21.4 say the same thing about the same slice.
   - NOT extrapolable. §21.4 established Sigma_t df_t^2 is not scale-free,
     so every figure here names its N and none of them implies another.
   - NOT a claim about buildNoteGraph or expandKeyword. They are a keyword
     tree for ONE note, they are not this function, and 4.4 does not touch
     them.
