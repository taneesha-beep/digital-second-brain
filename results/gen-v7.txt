==============================================================================
G. THE CORRELATION — THE READING OF 5.7 WITH STATISTICAL CONTENT
==============================================================================

  DENOMINATOR: SEED-ARM OBSERVATIONS. One per seed per arm that delivered.

  Does a seed whose neighbours were retrieved BETTER produce better items?
  Two arms is two points; this pools every seed in both arms, which both
  doubles n and puts the retrieval axis over its full range.

  TWO POOLED FIGURES, AND THE SECOND IS THE ONE TO QUOTE.

  A NAIVE POOL CONFOUNDS TWO SOURCES OF VARIATION AND ONLY ONE OF THEM HAS
  n=46 BEHIND IT. Both arms differ in mean nDCG@8 AND in mean outcome, so
  pooling their raw values lets a single BETWEEN-ARM contrast — which is n=2,
  two arm means — masquerade as 46 observations. That is the shape of
  Simpson's paradox, and here it is not hypothetical: lexical support
  disagrees between the pooled and per-arm readings below.

  WITHIN-ARM centres each variable on its own arm's mean before pooling, so
  the estimate uses only variation BETWEEN SEEDS INSIDE an arm — the question
  actually being asked. It is the fixed-effects estimate and it is the honest
  pooled number.

    outcome                     pool     n     pearson r   95% CI             spearman  |r| detectable
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    mean lexical support         naive        46   -0.273       [-0.522, 0.018]   -0.201     0.290
                                 within-arm   46   -0.255       [-0.508, 0.038]   -0.270     0.290   <- QUOTE THIS
    mean judged level            naive        46   -0.321       [-0.559, -0.033]   -0.354     0.290
                                 within-arm   46   -0.303       [-0.545, -0.014]   -0.316     0.290   <- QUOTE THIS
    rate(2)                      naive        46   -0.285       [-0.531, 0.006]   -0.307     0.290
                                 within-arm   46   -0.252       [-0.505, 0.041]   -0.288     0.290   <- QUOTE THIS

  AND PER ARM, so a pooled figure cannot hide two arms disagreeing:

    arm      outcome                  n    pearson r   spearman
    ------------------------------------------------------------
    gen-v5   mean lexical support      23   0.047       0.010
    gen-v5   mean judged level         23   -0.429       -0.463
    gen-v7   mean lexical support      23   -0.386       -0.324
    gen-v7   mean judged level         23   -0.200       -0.222

  BOTH r AND rho ARE PRINTED BECAUSE THE RETRIEVAL AXIS IS MOSTLY TIES. With
  a median of one judged document per seed, most seeds sit at nDCG@8 = 0, and
  Pearson on a near-binary predictor is a two-group comparison wearing a
  correlation's clothes. Spearman with average ranks is the honest reading;
  they are reported together so a reader can see when they disagree.

==============================================================================
H. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
==============================================================================

  NO NOISE FLOOR UNDER EITHER ARM. One draw per seed at temperature 0.4, no
  repeats, in both arms alike. §28.8 measured 32.1% of examQs cells flipping
  verdict on a re-draw. A between-arm difference smaller than the re-draw
  flip rate is not attributable to the retriever, and NOTHING here or in 5.4
  establishes what that rate is for a study pack. This is the limitation most
  likely to be quoted past, because every figure above is a clean number.

  THE GROUNDEDNESS RATE HAS NO HUMAN VALIDATION IN EITHER ARM. §33.8: the
  human never used the top level in 60 items, so the binary kappa is exactly
  0.000 with a degenerate marginal. The second arm collects no hand labels by
  pre-registration, and its figures carry 5.6's three-level kappa of 0.246 at
  P_o 64.0% by TRANSFER — the instrument is frozen across arms, but transfer
  is an assumption and not a measurement.

  THE RETRIEVAL AXIS IS SPARSE AND NEARLY BINARY. Median one judged document
  per seed. A per-seed nDCG@8 answers "was the linked question found", which
  is a coarser question than "how good were these eight neighbours".

  ASSIGNMENT IS BY DESIGN, SO THE ARM CONTRAST IS CAUSAL; THE PER-SEED
  CORRELATION IS OBSERVATIONAL. A seed's retrievability may be confounded
  with properties of the seed nobody here measured.

  THE CLUSTERS ARE NOT APP-SHAPED. Neighbours come from all 27,325 corpus
  documents rather than a <=500-note user slice, and the seeds are Stack
  Exchange questions shaped as Notes. §12.2's gap, eleventh instance.

  30 SEEDS IS NOT 2,304 QUERIES. The ladder's separations were established on
  the dev split; this draw reproduces their direction, not their magnitudes.
