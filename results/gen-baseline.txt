  REASONING TOKENS — A CONFOUND ON SECTION D, MEASURED RATHER THAN NAMED
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  openai/gpt-oss-120b emits a reasoning chain in a SEPARATE
  `reasoning` field, so it does NOT pollute the JSON and section B is clean.
  But reasoning tokens COUNT AGAINST completion_tokens and therefore against
  max_tokens: 1024 — so they consume ceiling that a non-reasoning model would
  have spent on content, and section D's truncation rate is inflated by
  exactly this much relative to one.

  feature      calls   reasoning(mean)  (p95)   share of the 1024 ceiling
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  summarize       30               63     157     6.2%
  flashcards      30              293     516    28.7%
  concepts        30              153     396    14.9%
  examQs          30              215     503    21.0%
  eli5            30              126     342    12.3%
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  ALL            150              170     466    16.6%

  SO SECTION D IS AN UPPER BOUND on what the shipped prompts would truncate
  at on a non-reasoning model, and the size of the overstatement is the last
  column. This is the confound priced, not the confound avoided — which is
  the better outcome, because avoiding it would have meant choosing a model
  for the measurement's convenience rather than the app's.

H. WITHIN-CELL VARIANCE — WHAT n=3 BOUGHT, AND WHETHER IT WAS WORTH IT
==============================================================================

  A cell is one (seed, feature). At temperature 0.4 repeated draws can
  disagree. THIS IS THE NUMBER 5.5 NEEDS: if cells flip on a re-draw, a small
  before/after difference is not attributable to the fix.

  n=3 WAS PLANNED AND THE QUOTA SET n INSTEAD. The run stopped on Groq's
  200,000 tokens-per-day cap after a complete n=1 pass plus part of the second,
  so NO CELL REACHED THREE DRAWS. The rows below are over cells with TWO, which
  answers the same question with less resolution: two draws can disagree, and
  the rate at which they do is what bounds 5.5's attributable difference.

  feature      cells n>=2   both conform   both fail   SPLIT
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  flashcards           28             28           0       0
  concepts             28             27           0       1
  examQs               28             11           8       9
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  ALL                  84   unanimous 74   SPLIT 10    11.9% of cells

  A SPLIT CELL IS A CELL WHOSE CONFORMANCE IS NOT A PROPERTY OF ITS INPUT.
  Read the ALL row as a floor on the noise 5.5 has to clear: with two draws
  the observed split rate understates the three-draw rate, because three draws
  have more chances to disagree.
