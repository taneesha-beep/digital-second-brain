  was in the dump the model trained on. A QUERY is UNSEEN only if its WHOLE key

  available, and per-query nDCG reads the whole key at once. Ambiguity is
  resolved toward SEEN, which is the stratum that cannot flatter the result.

  THE COMPARISON IS OF THE DIFFERENCE WITHIN EACH STRATUM, NEVER OF THE ABSOLUTE
  LEVELS ACROSS THEM. §5.3 measured that per-query scores are not comparable
  across corpus age, because a document's judged degree tracks how much corpus
  was created after it. Both absolute columns move between strata for that
  reason, and that movement is §5.3's effect rather than a finding here.

  AND IT REMOVES MEMORISATION OF THE RELATION, NOT EXPOSURE TO THE TEXT. The
  same model card lists 25,316,456 Stack Exchange (Title, Body) pairs, so both
  documents in a post-snapshot pair were very likely seen individually. What is
  supported is "the margin does not depend on having been shown this pair".
