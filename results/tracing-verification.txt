⚠️  EVERY DURATION BELOW IS UNCONTROLLED AND IS NOT A LATENCY FIGURE.
    One laptop, one process, no concurrency, no warmup, no repeats, n=1, and a
    third-party API call over a home network in the middle of it. ROADMAP 6.5 —
    which would have owned a CONTROLLED figure — is CUT, and EVALUATION §35.5a
    makes that permanent: this project will never publish a controlled latency
    number. "Uncontrolled" here is a FINAL STATE, not a placeholder. These
    numbers show STRUCTURE — what nests inside what, and which bar dominates —
    and they must never be quoted as performance.
