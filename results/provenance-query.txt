3. WHAT THE NUMBERS DO NOT SAY
   - docs examined == returned means the index answered without touching
     a row it did not need. It does NOT mean the query is cheap in any
     absolute sense, and no budget for it has been stated anywhere.
   - The wall times include driver and BSON decode for every returned row,
     so the large answers are dominated by materialising them rather than
     by finding them. That is why keys/docs examined is the load-bearing
     column and the milliseconds are context.
   - Loopback to a container. No Atlas, no replica set, no real network.
   - The row mixture is synthetic and uniform. A real store after a
     partial re-save is neither.
