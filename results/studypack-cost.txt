C. WHERE THE MONEY GOES, AND IT IS NOT WHERE THE TOKENS ARE
==============================================================================

  input is  43.4% of the TOKENS but 16.1% of the COST
  output is 56.6% of the TOKENS but 83.9% of the COST
  because output is priced 4.0x input.

  AND MOST OF THE OUTPUT IS REASONING NOBODY EVER READS:

    reasoning tokens        33,535  of 54,957 output  (61.0%)
    priced at            $0.020121  of $0.039292  (51.2% of the bill)

  gpt-oss-120b is a reasoning model and Groq bills reasoning tokens inside
  completion_tokens at the output rate. They are not returned to the user
  and no metric in this repository scores them. Reported because a cost
  attribution that hides the majority of its own bill is not an attribution.

==============================================================================
D. WHY THIS IS A LOWER BOUND, NOT AN ESTIMATE
==============================================================================

  finished "length"      7 of 30  (23.3%)
  finished "stop"        23 of 30

    mean cost, truncated $0.001455
    mean cost, completed $0.001266

  THE LEDGER IS CENSORED AT EXACTLY THE QUANTITY BEING PRICED. 7 of 30 calls
  hit the 2048-token ceiling and stopped mid-output. Those calls would have
  emitted MORE output tokens if allowed to finish, and output is the
  expensive side — so the true mean is HIGHER than the figure in section B,
  by an amount this run structurally cannot report. EVALUATION §35.3 makes
  the general form of this argument: every run is censored at exactly the
  quantity being estimated, which is why 5.9's new ceiling was PICKED and
  not DERIVED.

  AND THE SHIPPED CEILING IS NO LONGER 2048. It is 4096 (5.9, for the study
  pack only; llm.service.js still holds 2048). So section B prices a
  CONFIGURATION THAT IS NO LONGER SHIPPED. Re-pricing needs a fresh 30-call
  run at 4096 — the same run EVALUATION §35.3 says the post-change
  truncation rate needs, and it has not been made.

  Two smaller reasons the real charge is at or below this figure:
    - Groq applies automatic prompt caching to this model at half rate on
      cached input. Nothing here observes cache hits, so every input token
      is priced at the full rate.
    - The free tier charges quota, not money. Quota is enforced on ACTUAL
      tokens (EVALUATION §30.1), which is what this prices.
