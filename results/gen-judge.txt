==============================================================================
B-F. WITHHELD — THE HAND LABELS ARE NOT IN YET
==============================================================================

  0 of 60 pre-registered hand labels exist.

  Every rate is withheld until they do. Cohen's kappa is a number about two
  INDEPENDENT raters, and a rater who has read the judge's verdicts — even as
  an aggregate tally — is not one. "I did not let it influence me" is exactly
  the claim that cannot be checked afterwards, so the display is closed rather
  than the discipline being trusted. The runner withholds them too; a guarantee
  that holds on one of two display paths is not a guarantee.

  NOTHING IS LOST. The ledger is written in full regardless, and this reporter
  is PURE — re-running it once the labels are in costs nothing.

    Label them:   cd backend && npm run judge:label

==============================================================================
H. WHAT THESE NUMBERS CANNOT SAY
==============================================================================

  KAPPA MEASURES AGREEMENT, NOT CORRECTNESS. One human, no inter-human
  agreement figure, so rubric ambiguity and rater error are not separable.

  THE ITEM SET IS CENSORED AT THE CALL LEVEL, AND THIS IS THE LIMITATION MOST
  LIKELY TO BE QUOTED PAST. gen-v5's 30 completed calls include 7 that
  truncated at the INHERITED max_tokens: 2048 ceiling. A truncated pack parses
  to nothing and contributes ZERO items, and those packs are systematically
  the LONGER seeds: 140.4 mean words against 87.3. So groundedness is
  measured over 23 of 30 seeds, skewed short. The items themselves are
  intact — every contributing call finished with `stop` — but the seed mix is
  not the golden set's. ROADMAP 5.9 owns the ceiling.

  NO NOISE FLOOR UNDER THE ITEMS. Inherited from gen-v5 and not fixed here:
  one draw per seed at temperature 0.4, no repeats. §28.8 measured 32.1% of
  examQs cells flipping verdict on a re-draw and §32.10 states that no gen-v5
  figure may be compared against a later one until repeats are bought. The
  JUDGE runs at temperature 0, so it adds little of its own — but temperature 0
  is not determinism, and nothing here claims a judged run would replay exactly.

  CORRELATED ERROR IS NOT RULED OUT BY A DIFFERENT VENDOR. Two models trained
  on overlapping text can be confidently wrong about the same claim. A
  different family lowers self-preference; it does not eliminate shared priors.

  NOT A RETRIEVER COMPARISON. That is 5.7, and the stamped retriever/digest on
  every gen-v5 ledger row is what it will need.

  THE NEIGHBOURS ARE RETRIEVED OVER 27,325 CORPUS DOCUMENTS, not a <=500-note
  user slice, and the seeds are Stack Exchange questions shaped as Notes.
  §12.2's gap, tenth instance.
