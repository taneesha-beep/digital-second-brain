PHASE 5.6 — PREDICTIONS, WRITTEN BEFORE THE FIRST JUDGE API CALL
================================================================================

Committed in its own commit so the ordering is checkable in git, the same way
results/gen-predictions.txt (5.3), results/gen-v2-predictions.txt (5.5) and
results/gen-v5-predictions.txt (5.4) were.

Priors, so the hit rate below is read against something: 5.4 finished 9 right /
4 wrong / 0 untestable (its own PROVISIONAL score on 9 of 30 seeds was 6/7, and
three rows flipped when the set completed — see §32.9), 5.5 scored 9/6/0, 5.1
3/3/1, 5.3 9 of 16, the README pass 4 of 6, and 4.6 5 of 10.

WHAT IS BEING PREDICTED. An LLM judge, qwen/qwen3.6-27b, grades whether each
Study Pack item is supported by a passage it is shown. The items are the 322
that gen-v5 produced (results/gen-v5.calls.jsonl). Each item is judged TWICE
under identical presentation:

  cited    the passage is the note the item actually cited
  null     the passage is one OTHER note from the SAME prompt, drawn uniformly
           at random with a fixed seed

644 judged pairs. The judge is never told which condition it is in.

THE DENOMINATORS ARE NOT ONE NUMBER, AND THAT IS §32's CENTRAL FINDING APPLIED
IN ADVANCE. 5.4 was simultaneously well-powered for items (322) and badly
under-powered for calls (30) and printed both under one seed count. Every band
below therefore names its own denominator:

  items (cited)      322     rows 1, 2, 12, 15
  items (paired)     322     row 3
  items (human)       50     rows 4, 5, 6, 7, 13
  items (human null)  10     row 14
  judge calls        644     rows 8, 9, 10, 11, 16

n IS DECLARED HERE AND NOT SET BY THE QUOTA. 644 calls at ~445 ACTUAL tokens is
~287,000 of a 200,000/day organisation cap — about 34 hours of refill at the
measured 2.3148 tokens/s, so this is a TWO-SESSION run by construction rather
than by accident. The emission order is stratum-major round-robin over the ten
(quintile x slot) strata with each item's two conditions emitted back to back,
so any stop leaves a set that is BALANCED and PAIRED rather than a prefix —
§32.8's defect, fixed on this axis by design instead of survived by luck.

ROW 1 IS THE HEADLINE AND IT IS DELIBERATELY LOW. THE ARGUMENT, BEFORE THE RUN.

§32.5 measured mean lexical containment against the cited note at 0.283, with
only 5.9% of items above the pre-committed 0.5 cut and 0.0% above 0.7, and drew
the conclusion that matters here: THE DOMINANT REGIME IS PARAPHRASE. A model
asked to "connect or contrast two notes over items that restate one" writes
abstractively, so containment mostly measures how little the model copies.

A rubric whose top level requires that EVERY factual assertion be stated in the
passage or follow directly from it punishes exactly that behaviour. So the
prediction is not that the feature is broken; it is that a strict groundedness
rubric and an abstractive generator disagree by construction, and the size of
that disagreement is the thing worth measuring.

TWO PROBE ITEMS SUPPORT THIS AND THEY ARE NOT EVIDENCE OF A RATE. Drawn by
LENGTH (median and p90 of claim+passage characters) rather than cherry-picked
while characterising the judge's token cost, both came back 0, and both look
right by hand: one item cited a note that only ASKS a question and never answers
it, and one attached a generic definition of "thermal death time" to a duck
confit question. n=2 is an existence proof, not a rate — §30.8's label, which
§32.2 records being ignored for exactly this kind of number.

ROW 15 IS THE ONE THAT TESTS WHETHER THE TWO METRICS SEE THE SAME THING.
§32.5 reports the cited note is the lexical argmax on 77.3% of items and calls
the remaining 22.7% "the pile 5.6 will have to sort" — some mis-attributions,
some correct citations of a note that phrased things differently, and the
lexical metric cannot tell them apart. If judged groundedness is materially
lower on that 22.7%, the judge is sorting the pile. If it is not, then either
the judge is measuring something else or the pile is mostly the benign case.

ROW 7 IS A PREDICTION ABOUT THE INSTRUMENT RATHER THAN THE SYSTEM. Cohen's
kappa collapses toward 0 under skewed marginals even at high raw agreement. If
rows 1 and 13 are right, both raters put most items in one or two categories,
which is precisely that regime. Reporting kappa alone would then understate
agreement, which is why P_o, P_e, the full confusion matrix and both marginals
print beside it. A groundedness rate without kappa beside it is a thing CLAUDE.md
lists under Claim discipline as never to write; a kappa without P_o beside it is
the same error one level down.

ROWS 10 AND 11 ARE A JUDGE-SPECIFIC COST FIGURE AND THAT IS THE POINT.
§30.1 measured actual/reserved at 0.40 for single-note calls; §32.2 measured
0.94 for study packs and established that THE RATIO IS A PROPERTY OF THE
FEATURE, not of the API — it is how much of max_tokens the feature uses. 5.4
priced a cluster run at the single-note ratio and stopped at 9 of 30 because of
it. So neither figure is inherited here: the bands come from six probe calls
against the judge model itself, at 389-534 prompt tokens and 11-14 completion
tokens with reasoning disabled.


WHAT WOULD MAKE THESE UNSCOREABLE

If the run stops before the ledger is complete, every row is scored on what
landed and LABELLED PROVISIONAL, with the fraction stated. §32.9 is the record
of why that label is load-bearing: 5.4's provisional 6-right/7-wrong on a third
of its set became 9-right/4-wrong on the whole of it, and three rows flipped.
Two separate partial sets in this project have now produced headlines their own
completions overturned (§29.5's, §32.9's).

Rows 4-7 and 13-14 need the hand labels. They are collected BEFORE the first
judge call, which is a sequencing decision rather than a discipline one: a human
who has seen the judge's answer is no longer an independent rater, and no
instruction can undo that once it has happened.


WHAT THESE NUMBERS WILL NOT BE ABLE TO SAY, WHATEVER THEY COME OUT AT

NO NOISE FLOOR, INHERITED FROM gen-v5 AND NOT FIXED HERE. The 322 items are one
draw at temperature 0.4 with no repeats; §28.8 measured 32.1% of examQs cells
flipping verdict on a re-draw and §32.10 states that no gen-v5 figure may be
compared against a later one until repeats are bought. The judge runs at
temperature 0, so the JUDGE's own contribution to variance is small, but the
ITEMS it grades are a single sample.

THE ITEM SET IS CENSORED AT THE CALL LEVEL. The 7 packs that truncated at the
inherited 2048 ceiling parse to nothing and contribute ZERO items, and they are
systematically the longer seeds: 140.4 mean words against 87.3, and Q5 keeps
only 3 of its 6 seeds where Q1 keeps 6 of 6. So groundedness is measured on 23
of 30 seeds, skewed short. The items themselves are intact — every contributing
call finished with `stop` — but the seed mix is not the golden set's.

CITATION SUPPORT (§32.5) AND GROUNDEDNESS ARE NOT THE SAME QUANTITY and rows 12
and 15 are the only places they are compared on purpose.

NOT A RETRIEVER COMPARISON. That is 5.7.
