Every rung predicted within +/-0.010 of its dev value. The splits are a uniform

therefore about the INTERVAL, not about a p.

4. THE HOLM OUTCOME
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Family of seven, alpha = 0.05, bound alpha/7 = 0.00714 at the smallest step.
Six p-values are on dev and one on test; the decision to correct the family AS
REGISTERED rather than re-register it on test is recorded in the writeup, with
the rejected option.

Predicted: v2->v3, v3->v4, v4->v5 and the cap ablation all survive Holm (four
at or near the bootstrap floor). v5->v6 survives as a LOSS — 0.0015 has to
clear alpha/3 = 0.01667 at its step position, which it does with room, but this
is the only member whose outcome is not knowable by inspection. v1->v2 does not
survive. sweep-tuned-vs-shipped does not survive.

So: 5 of 7 predicted to survive, one of them a loss.
