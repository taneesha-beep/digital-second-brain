WHAT IS BEING PREDICTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Study Pack run over the SAME 30 golden seeds under a SECOND retriever, and the
resulting items judged by the SAME judge under the SAME frozen rubric, so that
retrieval quality (nDCG@8, against the Stack Exchange qrels) and downstream
generation quality can be put in one table.

  arm A   gen-v5 + gen-judge   v4-bm25         ALREADY BOUGHT, not re-run
  arm B   gen-v7 + judge-v7    v5-embeddings   what this phase spends

THE RETRIEVER IS THE ONLY VARIABLE. Held fixed: the 30 seeds, the five control
prompts, the Study Pack prompt, openai/gpt-oss-120b, temperature 0.4,
max_tokens 2048, CONTEXT_TOKEN_BUDGET 1800, k=8, the corpus sha; and on the
judging side qwen/qwen3.6-27b, temperature 0, reasoning_effort 'none',
max_tokens 128, scripts/lib/judge-rubric.js, scripts/lib/judge-metrics.js,
scripts/lib/gen-schema.js, scripts/lib/studypack-metrics.js and the distractor
draw rule. §29.4's same-grader guarantee, one phase on.


THE RETRIEVAL HALF WAS MEASURED BEFORE THESE PREDICTIONS WERE WRITTEN, AND
SAYING SO IS THE POINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

nDCG@8 per seed costs no quota, so the retrieval side of the table existed
before the design was fixed and WAS USED TO FIX IT. That is a design input, not
an outcome, and the honest record is that arm B was chosen because of it:

  v4-bm25 minus the candidate, paired over the 30 seeds, nDCG@8

    v1-overlap       -0.072   t = -1.30   18 of 30 seeds TIED   2.20/8 shared
    v5-embeddings    +0.138   t =  2.09   11 of 30 seeds tied   1.87/8 shared
    v6-hybrid        +0.114   t =  2.20   16 of 30 seeds tied   4.03/8 shared

ROADMAP 5.7 SAYS "v1 versus the winning retriever" AND THIS RUNS v4 VERSUS THE
WINNER INSTEAD. The departure is deliberate and it is the never-change-two-
variables rule applied to the INPUT side: on this 30-seed sample v1 and v4 are
not measurably different (t = -1.30 over 30 seeds, and t = -0.39 over the 23
that survive the output ceiling), so a v1 arm would spend ~456,000 tokens
varying an independent variable that does not vary on the sample it is varied
on. v5-embeddings is the ladder winner, gives roughly double the contrast, and
shares the fewest neighbours with arm A.

EVERY PREDICTION BELOW IS ABOUT A GENERATION OR JUDGING OUTCOME. None of them
is about the retrieval column, because that column is already known.

POWER, DECLARED BEFORE THE RUN RATHER THAN DISCOVERED AFTER IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Minimum detectable effect, 80% power, alpha .05, two-sample, computed on arm A's
own committed ledgers with the intra-seed clustering MEASURED (ICC 0.033 for
lexical support, 0.027 for the judged level, ~14 items per seed, design effect
1.42 and 1.36):

  metric                    mean     MDE            MDE as % of the mean
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  rate(2), cited            5.0%     5.60 points    113%   <- CANNOT SPEAK
  judged mean level (0-2)   0.202    0.132           65%   <- weak
  lexical support           0.283    0.031           11%   <- the powered one

SO THE HEADLINE OF 5.7 IS THE CORRELATION, NOT THE BETWEEN-ARM DIFFERENCE, and
that is a pre-registered choice rather than a retreat after a null. Two arms is
two points and two points have no correlation; the quantity with content is the
PER-SEED correlation between nDCG@8 and downstream quality, pooled to ~46
seed-arm observations where a |r| of 0.29 is detectable.

RATE(2) IS REPORTED AND IS PRE-DECLARED INCAPABLE OF SHOWING A DIFFERENCE. Its
MDE exceeds its own mean, so a null on row 9 is not evidence of no difference
and must never be written up as one. §33.8: the human never used the top level
in 60 items and the binary kappa is exactly 0.000, so the 5.0% has no human
validation either. Nothing in this phase repairs that and nothing here re-scales
the rubric — that would be fitting a rubric to results.

AND A BIGGER SAMPLE DOES NOT RESCUE THE JUDGED BETWEEN-ARM CONTRAST. Detecting
the effect implied by arm A's own correlation needs roughly 8,450 items per arm,
about 600 seeds per arm — 20x this design. That is out of reach permanently, not
just this month.

ROW 12 IS THE ONE CARRYING REAL RISK, AND IT IS AN OUT-OF-SAMPLE REPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Arm A's own committed ledgers already contain a per-seed correlation between
nDCG@8 and mean judged level of -0.429 (Spearman -0.463) over its 23 delivering
seeds. That is NEGATIVE: the seeds where retrieval did better are the seeds
where the judge scored the generated items lower. Group means rather than an
outlier — 13 seeds at nDCG@8 = 0 average a judged level of 0.269, and the 10
with nDCG@8 > 0 average 0.114.

It is not a length artefact. nDCG@8 against seed word count is r = 0.185 and the
judged level against word count is r = -0.101, so the obvious confound does not
carry it.

BUT AT n = 23 THE DETECTABLE |r| IS 0.412 AND THIS IS 0.429, so it sits on the
edge, it is one test among several, and it has no correction applied. Row 12
predicts it REPLICATES in a differently-retrieved arm. If it does, "better
retrieval produces better generation" is false on this system and that is the
finding. If it does not, the arm A figure was noise and the honest write-up
says so.

The mechanism I would offer if it replicates, stated now so it cannot be
invented afterwards: a seed whose linked duplicate IS retrieved gets a
near-duplicate neighbour, the prompt asks for items that "connect or contrast"
rather than restate, and a strict groundedness rubric punishes exactly that.
§32.5's paraphrase finding, one level up. That is a story, not a measurement,
and n=46 cannot separate it from the alternative.

ROW 13 PREDICTS THE TWO DOWNSTREAM METRICS DISAGREE, WHICH THEY ALREADY DO IN
ARM A: lexical support is 0.283 on the nDCG=0 seeds and 0.282 on the nDCG>0
seeds — flat to three places — while the judged level moves by a factor of 2.4
across the same split. If rows 12 and 13 both land, the phase's real result is
that groundedness and lexical containment are not measuring the same thing, and
§32.5's "proxy" label is understated.


WHY MOST OF THE BANDS PREDICT NO MOVEMENT, AND WHY THAT IS NOT HEDGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.6 scored 7/9 and SIX of the nine wrong rows were ONE assumption spent six
times: that the top rubric level would fire at a moderate rate, when §32.5 had
already measured, one phase earlier, why it would not. The failure was not
optimism; it was spending a single un-rechecked belief across most of the
sheet.

So the assumption most at risk here — that a better retriever moves the
downstream metrics at all — is the one I am deliberately NOT making. Arm A's own
data says lexical support is flat across the retrieval-quality split. Rows 7 and
11 therefore predict differences INSIDE their own MDEs, which is a prediction
that the experiment will return a null, made in advance, with the null's size
stated so it cannot later be dressed up as a finding.

And the rows are spread across four independent beliefs on purpose — retrieval
and truncation mechanics (1, 2, 3), harness cost and delivery (14, 15, 16, 17),
outcome levels (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10), and the correlation (7, 11, 12, 13) — so one
wrong belief cannot take six rows with it the way it did at 5.6.


WHAT WOULD MAKE THESE UNSCOREABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If either run stops before its ledger is complete, every row is scored on what
landed and LABELLED PROVISIONAL, with the fraction stated. §32.9 is the record
of why that label is load-bearing: 5.4's provisional 6-right/7-wrong on a third
of its set became 9-right/4-wrong on the whole of it, and three rows flipped.
Two separate partial sets in this project have produced headlines their own
completions overturned (§29.5, §32.9).

The judge emission order is stratified so any stop leaves a BALANCED and PAIRED
sample rather than a prefix, re-derived for this phase's axis rather than
inherited from 5.6 — §32.8's lesson, which has now recurred on four axes.

Rows 12 and 13 need enough seeds in BOTH arms. Below about 16 paired seeds the
pooled correlation's own interval is wider than the band and the rows are marked
UNTESTABLE rather than scored against a number that cannot support them.


WHAT THESE NUMBERS WILL NOT BE ABLE TO SAY, WHATEVER THEY COME OUT AT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NO NOISE FLOOR UNDER EITHER ARM. One draw per seed at temperature 0.4, no
repeats, in arm A and in arm B alike. §28.8 measured 32.1% of examQs cells
flipping verdict on a re-draw and §32.10 forbids comparing a gen-v5 figure
against a later one until repeats are bought. A between-arm difference smaller
than the re-draw flip rate is not attributable to the retriever, and NOTHING
here establishes what that rate is for a study pack.

BOTH ARMS ARE CENSORED AT THE CALL LEVEL BY THE 2048 CEILING, and the censoring
is not neutral for this comparison. Arm A's 7 truncated packs are the longer
seeds and they are also where the retrievers separate most: v4 scores 0.274 on
them against v1's 0.038, so the ceiling eats 69% of the v1 contrast and 21% of
the v5 one. That attenuation is the number ROADMAP 5.9 inherits from this phase.
Holding the ceiling fixed keeps this a one-variable change; it does not make the
censoring go away, and pairing on seeds that deliver in both arms is what
neutralises it.

THE CLUSTERS ARE NOT APP-SHAPED. Neighbours come from all 27,325 corpus
documents, not a <=500-note user slice, and the seeds are Stack Exchange
questions shaped as Notes. §12.2's gap, eleventh instance.

THE GROUNDEDNESS RATE STILL HAS NO HUMAN VALIDATION. 5.6's binary kappa is
0.000 with a degenerate marginal, and no hand labels are collected for arm B.
Any groundedness figure from this phase carries 5.6's kappa beside it or is not
quoted at all.

CORRELATION IS NOT CAUSATION AND n=46 IS SMALL. The retriever is assigned by
design, so the between-arm contrast is causal; the per-seed correlation is
observational and a seed's retrievability may be confounded with properties of
the seed nobody here measured.
